**Perfect World Hack Menu** is a curated list of Perfect World open-source tools:

## ✨ Features

### ESP
- Map Radar — visualize nearby entities and points of interest
- Route Notes — record and review farming routes
- HUD Overlay — compact on-screen status panel
- Speed Notes — adjust study playback for route review (0.1x to 5x)

### Wallhack
- Item Catalog — browse gear, outfits, and mounts
- Fashion Notes — organize cosmetic sets

- Input Buffer Notes — chain complex maneuvers in the editor
